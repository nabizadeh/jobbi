=====================================
SUBAGENT STEP 8 — GENERATE COVER LETTER
=====================================
First, check COVER_LETTER in profile.md.
If it is NOT set to "yes", stop immediately — do not generate a cover letter.

If it IS set to "yes": generate a cover letter for this specific job.
Output: cover_letter.md in the job folder.

=====================================
STEP 8a — CHECK FOR WRITING SAMPLE
=====================================
Before writing, scan the resumes/ folder for a file named writing_sample.txt or
writing_sample.md. If found, read it and note the candidate's natural writing style:
- Sentence length and rhythm
- Level of formality
- How they open and close
- Punctuation and paragraph habits
- Any recurring phrases or structural patterns

Use this style as a guide throughout the cover letter. Do not copy sentences — just
match the tone and cadence. If no writing sample is found, default to clear, direct,
professional-but-human prose.

=====================================
STEP 8b — WRITE THE COVER LETTER
=====================================
Structure (3–4 short paragraphs, under 250 words total):

1. Opening (2–3 sentences):
   - Do NOT start with "I am writing to express my interest in..."
   - Do NOT open with a compliment about the company
   - Lead with a specific, concrete thing from the candidate's background
     that is directly relevant to this role
   - Then connect it briefly to why this role makes sense

2. Body (1–2 paragraphs):
   - Pick the 2–3 most relevant experiences or achievements from the resume
   - Be specific — name the actual work, result, or method, not a vague claim
   - Pull one specific signal from the job description (a technology, a domain,
     a stated priority) and connect it to the candidate's actual experience
   - Do NOT restate the job description back at them

3. Closing (2–3 sentences):
   - Express genuine interest without overselling
   - Do NOT write "I would be a great fit" or "I look forward to hearing from you"
   - Do NOT write "Please do not hesitate to contact me"
   - End simply and directly — a one-line statement of interest and availability is enough

=====================================
STEP 8c — ANTI-AI RULES (HARD)
=====================================
These phrases are banned. If any appear in your draft, rewrite that sentence:

BANNED PHRASES:
- "I am passionate about"
- "I am excited about this opportunity"
- "I would be a great fit"
- "I am writing to express my interest"
- "I am highly skilled in"
- "I have a proven track record"
- "I thrive in fast-paced environments"
- "I am a team player"
- "I look forward to hearing from you"
- "Please do not hesitate to contact me"
- "I am confident that"
- "Leveraging my expertise"
- Any sentence that reads like a job description reworded as a claim

STYLE RULES:
- Short sentences preferred. Vary length — not every sentence the same rhythm.
- No paragraph longer than 3 sentences.
- Write like you're talking to a smart person, not performing for HR.
- One specific detail beats three generic claims every time.
- If a sentence could appear in ANY cover letter for ANY job, cut it or rewrite it.

=====================================
STEP 8d — WRITE OUTPUT FILE
=====================================
Write cover_letter.md to the job folder with this format:

---
**[Job Title] — [Company]**

[Cover letter body — plain paragraphs, no bullet points]

---
*Draft generated by jobbi. Read this aloud — anything that doesn't sound like you,
rewrite it before sending.*
